# Security Policy

This policy covers ZK Framework: the CE modules in this repository and the PE and EE modules, ZK themes and add-ons that
Potix Corporation distributes with them.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **security@potix.com**. Please do not report a vulnerability in a public issue, pull request or forum post.

Please include:

- the product, edition (CE, PE or EE) and version affected;
- what the vulnerability is and what an attacker can do with it;
- the steps, a proof of concept or a sample application that reproduces it;
- any workaround you know of.

## What happens next

We acknowledge receipt within 2 business days and work with you on coordinated disclosure.

Potix does not operate a bug bounty program or offer monetary rewards for vulnerability reports.
